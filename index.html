Welcome

A breath of calm, energy and change

Life moves fast. Between deadlines, devices and the daily noise, it's easy to lose touch with the one thing that's always with us — our breath. Miracle Breath is an invitation to come back to it.

Through the SOMA Breath method, we use rhythmic breathing, music and meditation to gently shift your body out of stress and into balance. It's accessible, it's grounding, and for many people it's genuinely transformative — whether you're seeking relief from overwhelm, deeper focus, or simply a moment to feel like yourself again.

Why breathwork

Small practice, profound benefits

Conscious breathing is one of the simplest, most portable wellbeing tools there is — and the effects can ripple through every part of life.

Calm & balance

Soothe a busy nervous system and find a steadier baseline, even on demanding days.

Energy & clarity

Oxygenate, awaken and sharpen focus — a natural lift without the caffeine crash.

Deeper rest

Wind-down practices quiet the mind so you sleep more easily and wake more restored.

Emotional release

Create space to process, soften tension and reconnect with a deeper sense of self.

Your guide

Hi, I'm Samantha

I'm the founder of Miracle Breath — a practice I created to help people access calm, healing and transformation through the power of conscious breathing.

My own journey into breathwork changed the way I move through the world, and SOMA Breath became the method I trusted most to share it. I'm a Level 2 SOMA certified instructor and hold an ADHD Awareness Diploma, and I'm passionate about creating a space where breath can support body, mind and a deeper sense of self.
