Welcome

A breath of calm, energy and change

Most of us breathe on autopilot all day, shoulders up by our ears, never quite catching up with ourselves. Miracle Breath is somewhere to put that down for an hour and actually breathe.

I teach the SOMA Breath method: simple rhythmic breathing set to music, with a little meditation woven through. It works whether you arrive frazzled and wired, or just want a reliable way to switch off. No experience required, and nothing you have to "get right".

Why breathwork

What a few minutes of breath can do

Conscious breathing is one of the simplest, most portable wellbeing tools there is — and the effects can ripple through every part of life.

Calm & balance

Soothe a busy nervous system and find a steadier baseline, even on demanding days.

Energy & clarity

Oxygenate, awaken and sharpen focus — a natural lift without the caffeine crash.

Deeper rest

Wind-down practices quiet the mind so you sleep more easily and wake more restored.

Emotional release

Create space to process, soften tension and reconnect with a deeper sense of self.

If this sounds like you

For the mind that won't switch off

You lie awake at 11pm with a head full of open tabs. You're tired but wired, running on coffee and to-do lists. Maybe focus feels slippery, or the world is just turned up too loud.

You're exactly who I built this for. I hold an ADHD Awareness Diploma, and my sessions are made for busy and sensitive minds. You won't be asked to sit perfectly still or empty your head. You just breathe, and let things go quiet for a while.
