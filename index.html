Good to know

Questions, answered

I've never done breathwork before — is that okay?

Absolutely. Most people arrive having never tried it. Every class is beginner-friendly, fully guided, and there's no special fitness or flexibility required. Just turn up as you are.

What should I bring or wear?

Wear something comfortable you can relax in. We provide mats, cushions, bolsters and blankets — but you're welcome to bring your own favourite. A water bottle is a good idea too.

Where exactly are you based?

Our studio is on the High Street in Kelvedon, Essex — easy to reach from Witham, Colchester and Braintree, with parking nearby. Full directions are sent when you book.

Is breathwork safe for everyone?

Breathwork is gentle and suitable for most people. If you're pregnant or have a heart condition, epilepsy, or a respiratory condition, just let us know in advance so we can adapt your practice and keep you safe.

How do I book?

Drop us a message through the form below or give us a call. Since Miracle Breath is a brand-new concept, we'd genuinely love to hear what would make it perfect for you.
