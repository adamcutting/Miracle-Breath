Getting found on Google

A free Google Business Profile puts Miracle Breath on Google Maps and in the box that appears when people search for breathwork near them. It's the single most useful thing for local bookings, and it costs nothing.

The short version
  1. Go to google.com/business and sign in (or create a free Google account with your Miracle Breath email).
  2. Add Miracle Breath, choose a category, and say you don't have a shop customers visit.
  3. List the towns you serve, add your phone and website.
  4. Verify the profile when Google asks.
  5. Add the description, photos and your classes, then ask happy clients for reviews.

1Create the profile

  1. Go to google.com/business and click Manage now.
  2. Sign in with a Google account. If you don't have one, choose Create account → For my personal use and use samantha@miraclebreath.co.uk (you don't need Gmail).
  3. Business name: Miracle Breath.
  4. Business category: start typing meditation and pick the closest match, such as Meditation instructor. You can add more categories later (for example Wellness program).
  5. When asked "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?", choose No. Prested Hall is a venue you use, not your own premises, so you're a business that serves an area.
  6. Service areas: add the places you'd like clients from, for example Feering, Kelvedon, Coggeshall, Witham, Colchester, Braintree and Chelmsford.
  7. Phone: 07867 725291. Website: https://miraclebreath.co.uk.
It's free. Google may offer adverts ("Google Ads") along the way. You can skip all of them; the profile doesn't need them.

2Verify it's really you

Google needs to check the business is real before the profile goes public. It will offer one or more options, usually a short video of you and your setup, a phone call or text, or an email. Follow its steps; it can take a few days. If it asks for a video, a minute showing your Miracle Breath logo, the room or equipment you use, and you is enough.

3Fill it in

Description. Copy and paste this (Google allows 750 characters), or write your own:

Business description
I'm Samantha, a certified SOMA Breath instructor with an ADHD Awareness Diploma. Miracle Breath offers guided breathwork and meditation to help you slow down, sleep better and feel more like yourself. Weekly classes run at Prested Hall in Feering on Tuesday lunchtimes and Thursday evenings, plus an online Sunday sleep session you can join from home. I also offer private one-to-one sessions and breathwork for workplaces. Every session is beginner-friendly and fully guided, and made with busy and sensitive minds in mind. No experience needed.
  • Photos: upload your logo, a photo of you, and a few of classes or the room at Prested Hall. Real photos of you make a big difference to how many people get in touch.
  • Hours: you can leave opening hours off, or set them to match the times you're happy to take calls.
  • Services: add Group breathwork classes, Online sleep session, Private 1:1 breathwork and Corporate wellbeing sessions.
  • Social links: add your Facebook and Instagram if Google offers the option.

4Reviews

Reviews are what most people look at first. Once the profile is live, find Ask for reviews on your profile, copy the link, and send it to clients who've enjoyed a class. A short, friendly message after a class works well. Reply to each review, even with a simple thank you.

5Keeping it fresh

  • When class times change, update them on your website and mention it in a Google post (Add update on your profile).
  • Add a new photo now and then. Profiles with recent photos tend to show up more.
