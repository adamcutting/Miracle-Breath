Updating your website

A quick guide for Samantha. You can change the words, photos and documents on miraclebreath.co.uk yourself, from your laptop or phone, at no cost.

The short version
  1. Go to app.pagescms.org and sign in with samantha@miraclebreath.co.uk.
  2. Click a page in the menu on the left.
  3. Change the words or photos.
  4. Press the green Save button at the top right.
  5. Your website updates by itself, usually within two minutes.

1Signing in

The first time, you'll get an invitation email from Pages CMS at samantha@miraclebreath.co.uk. Click the link in it and you're in.

After that, whenever you want to make a change:

  1. Go to app.pagescms.org (worth bookmarking).
  2. Type samantha@miraclebreath.co.uk in the Email box and press Continue with email.
  3. Open the sign-in email in your inbox and click the link.

You don't need a password, and you can ignore the "Sign in with GitHub" button. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

2Finding your way around

  • Pages: Home, About, SOMA Breath, Healing, Contact, and "Page not found" (shown when someone follows a broken link).
  • Site settings: your email address, phone number and the footer text that appears on every page.
  • Photos and Documents (PDF): everything you've uploaded, such as photos, your certificates and the waiver.

Grey notes under each box explain what it's for. If you see an "Actions" link, you can ignore it.

3Changing words

Click into any box and type, just like an email. The larger text boxes work like a simple word processor: select some words to make them bold or italic, and leave a blank line between paragraphs.

Gold italic headline: on the Home page, put *stars* around words in the headline to make them gold and italic, for example Breathwork for *healing* and calm.
Google & sharing at the top of each page is what shows in Google results and when the page is shared on WhatsApp or Facebook. Keep the title short (under about 60 characters).

4Saving

When you're happy, press Save at the top right. A message at the bottom of the screen confirms it saved.

Your website rebuilds itself and shows the change within about two minutes. Refresh the page in your browser to see it.

If you leave a page without pressing Save, your changes on that page are lost, so save as you go.

5Cards and lists

  • Click the little arrow to open an item and edit it.
  • Add an item adds a new card; the bin icon removes one.
  • Drag the dotted handle on the left to change the order.
  • Card icons and colours are picked from a drop-down list.

6Changing a photo

  1. Remove the old photo (bin icon), then press Upload or Select.
  2. Fill in Describe the photo with a few words, such as "Samantha leading a class by the sea". This helps people who use screen readers, and Google.
  3. Press Save.
Photos straight from your phone are fine. The website resizes them automatically. Wide (landscape) photos suit most places; the About portrait suits an upright photo. Only use photos you took yourself or have permission to use.

7Certificates and the waiver

Your certificates (About page) and the participation & waiver agreement (SOMA Breath and Healing pages) live in Documents (PDF). To replace one, for example a new version of the waiver:

  1. Open the page that links to it and find the box (for the waiver, the Health notice box).
  2. Under Linked document, remove the old file and upload the new PDF.
  3. Press Save. Do the same on the other page if it links to the same document.

8Your contact details

Site settings holds your email address, phone number and footer text. Change them once and they update everywhere on the site.

Messages sent through the website's enquiry form always arrive at samantha@miraclebreath.co.uk. A copy also goes to the address in Also send enquiries to (currently your Hotmail address); clear that box to stop the copies.

9If something goes wrong

You can't break the website for good. Every save is kept in a history, so any earlier version can be put back.

  • If something looks wrong after a change, or doesn't appear after five minutes, message Adam with the page and what you changed. He can undo it in a minute.
  • If a save ever fails, the previous version of the website simply stays live.

10What needs Adam

These are deliberately locked so nothing gets broken by accident:

  • The layout, colours and fonts, and the menu at the top.
  • Adding a brand-new page (for example, a timetable or blog).
  • The guided breathing exercise on the SOMA Breath page.
  • The enquiry form's questions (you can change the "I'm interested in…" choices).
