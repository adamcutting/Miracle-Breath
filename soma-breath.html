What is SOMA Breath?

A dynamic breathwork practice that brings together rhythmic breathing, music, meditation and visualisation to create a powerful mind-body experience.

The method

SOMA Breath is a dynamic breathwork practice that combines guided rhythmic breathing, music, meditation and visualisation. Inspired by ancient breathing traditions and shaped for modern life, it is designed to help improve focus, reduce stress, increase energy and support a deeper sense of inner balance.

How it works

Five elements, woven into one experience

Each session layers these together, gently guiding your body and mind into a deeply restorative state.

Rhythmic breathing

Structured, musical breathing patterns that energise the body and bring the nervous system into a balanced rhythm.

Breath retention

Comfortable, guided breath holds that train resilience and invite a profound sense of stillness and clarity.

Meditation

Moments of focused awareness that quiet mental chatter and deepen your connection to the present.

Music & sound

Carefully composed soundscapes that carry the practice, set the pace and help you drop into the experience.

Visualisation

Gentle guided imagery that channels intention, supports healing and leaves you feeling renewed.

Integration

Time to rest and absorb, so the calm and clarity you've cultivated can settle and stay with you.

Ancient wisdom, modern science

Time-honoured breath, understood anew

SOMA Breath draws on pranayama-based breathing techniques refined over thousands of years, and pairs them with our modern understanding of the body, the nervous system and the power of music and sound.

SOMA Breath was developed by Niraj Naik and is taught by certified instructors worldwide. The result is a practice that feels both grounding and credible — gentle enough for beginners, structured enough to trust, and designed to meet the realities of a busy life.

SOMA Breath® is a registered method; Samantha is a certified Level 2 SOMA instructor.

Important information

Breathwork is generally safe and gentle, but some techniques aren't suitable for everyone. If you are pregnant or have a heart condition, epilepsy, high blood pressure or a respiratory condition, please speak with Samantha and your GP before taking part. A full waiver and health guidance will be provided before your first session.
