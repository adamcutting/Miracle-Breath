My story

How Miracle Breath began

I'm Samantha. I found breathwork at a point when I was running on empty and not coping nearly as well as I was pretending to.

I tried it half expecting nothing. Instead I slept better, my head got quieter, and I slowly felt like myself again. It honestly felt a bit miraculous, which is where the name came from. I trained properly so I could offer the same thing to other people, without any of the woo that had put me off for years.

These days that's my favourite part of the week: a roomful of people, online or in person, all letting their shoulders drop at the same time.

— Samantha

What to expect

What it's actually like

No experience needed, no pressure, no judgement — just a supportive environment and the gentle guidance of your own breath.

Grounded & welcoming

Every session is beginner-friendly and fully guided. Come exactly as you are — curious, sceptical or somewhere in between.

Modern meets ancient

The work blends modern, evidence-informed breathing techniques with the calm and intention of ancient practice.

Inclusive & supportive

Especially mindful of neurodiversity, overwhelm and high-pressure lives — adaptable to how you feel on the day.

Your first time

What to expect in a session

A little reassurance, because the unknown is the hardest part. Here's roughly how it goes.

Around 45–75 minutes

Time to arrive, settle, breathe and rest. Never rushed.

Just wear comfy clothes

Lie down or sit. Mats and blankets provided in person; a quiet spot is all you need online.

Camera off is fine

Online sessions are camera-optional — keep yours off and simply follow my voice.

Emotion is welcome

Some people feel energised, some feel tearful, some drift off. All of it is completely normal.
