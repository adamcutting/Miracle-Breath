My story

How Miracle Breath began

Samantha Harden is the founder of Miracle Breath — a breathwork practice created to help people access calm, healing and transformation through the power of conscious breathing.

Like many people, Samantha came to breathwork during a season of life that asked a lot of her. What began as a personal tool for steadying the mind quickly became something far bigger: a doorway to more energy, clearer focus and a deeper, kinder relationship with herself. The change felt nothing short of miraculous — and the name Miracle Breath was born.

Today, Samantha shares that same practice with others, creating a warm, welcoming space where breath can support physical wellbeing, emotional balance and a deeper sense of self.

— Samantha
